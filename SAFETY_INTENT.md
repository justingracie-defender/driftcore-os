# DriftCore Safety Intent

DriftCore is intended to help keep people in control of systems that use it. The project asks adopters and contributors to preserve human oversight, honest auditability, and meaningful shutdown paths. These requests describe project intent only.

**This document is non-binding. It does not add conditions to, modify, or restrict the Apache License 2.0 grant.** The repository's legal license is the Apache License 2.0 in `LICENSE`; this document is not an additional license term.

## Name policy

The name **DriftCore** identifies this project's unmodified distribution and implementations that maintain the project's documented safety intent. Apache License 2.0 Section 6 does not grant trademark rights. Forks and modified distributions should use a distinct name and should not imply that DriftCore endorses or certifies a modified system.

## Project requests

If you build on DriftCore, we ask—but do not require—that you:

- keep meaningful human oversight and an effective halt path;
- preserve honest audit trails and document material safety limitations;
- avoid presenting this research software as certified life-safety equipment; and
- share safety improvements and reproducible findings where practical.

These are community and safety goals, not additional legal conditions on the Apache license.
